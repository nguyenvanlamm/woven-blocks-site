# Privacy Policy — Woven Blocks

Effective October 12, 2026

**Woven Blocks does not collect or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The iPhone, iPad and Android app makes no network requests.

## What stays on your device

Your game in progress, your statistics, your daily results and your settings (theme, sound, vibration) are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. In a web browser they are kept in that browser's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase your statistics in Settings → Reset statistics. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes everything.

## Sharing a result

When you tap Share, the game hands a short text (your score and streak) to your device's share sheet, or copies it to the clipboard in a browser. You choose where it goes; the game does not see or keep it.

## Playing in a web browser

The web version is downloaded from this site, including its graphics engine and fonts. It loads nothing from other servers and sends nothing anywhere while you play. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with any web page, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address; see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
