01Fair deal

You lose to your moves, never to luck.

Every tray of three you are dealt can be placed in full, in some order. If you get stuck, the order was the problem, not luck.

The game ends only when no piece fits anywhere, in any turn.

Fig. 1 — A tray. Tap a piece to turn it.

All three fit, in some order.
Turning is free: a quarter turn per tap.

02Score

The score shows its working.

Under every clear the game prints the sum that made it. This one is the move replayed at the top of the page: three cells placed, two rows cleared, combo ×3.

Scoring, in full

Each cell you place+1
Each clear10 × lines² × combo
Empty board, “Perfect”+300

Full rows and columns clear at the same time.

Keep the combo.

Every placement that clears raises the combo. Clear again within three placements or it resets. The fuse shows how many are left.

  1. Clear×33 moves left
  2. Place×32 moves left
  3. Place×31 move left
  4. Place—Combo resets
