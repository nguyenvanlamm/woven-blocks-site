01On your device

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, your statistics, your daily results and your settings (theme, sound, vibration) are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. In a web browser they are kept in that browser’s local storage.

They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase your statistics in Settings → Reset statistics. Deleting the app, or clearing this site’s data in your browser, removes everything.

02Sharing

Sharing a result

When you tap Share, the game hands a short text (your score and streak) to your device’s share sheet, or copies it to the clipboard in a browser. You choose where it goes; the game does not see or keep it.

03Web

Playing in a web browser

The web version is downloaded from this site, including its graphics engine and fonts. It loads nothing from other servers and sends nothing anywhere while you play.

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with any web page, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

04Children

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
