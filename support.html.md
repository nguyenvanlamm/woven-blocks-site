# Support — Woven Blocks

A fair block puzzle: every tray you are dealt can be placed. Found a bug or have a question? Write any time.

## How to play

- Tap a piece in the tray to turn it a quarter turn, then drag it onto the board.
- Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clearing several at once scores much more.
- Clear again within 3 placements to keep the combo going.
- Every set of three pieces can always be placed in some order. The game ends when no piece fits anywhere, in any turn.

## Questions

**Where is my progress saved?** On your device only. The game saves after every move, so closing it and coming back resumes the same board. Progress is not synced between devices.

**What is the daily challenge?** One board a day, the same for everyone. Your first run of the day is the one that counts toward your streak.

**How do I start over?** Settings → Reset statistics clears your scores and streaks. The restart button on the board deals a new classic game.

**Does it need the internet?** The iPhone, iPad and Android app plays offline. In a browser, the game needs a connection to load; it has no ads and no account either way.

**Is it free?** Yes. Woven Blocks is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

**When is it coming to iPhone, iPad and Android?** iPhone and iPad are coming soon to the App Store; Android later. Until then you can play in your browser.
