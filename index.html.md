# Woven Blocks: Every tray fits.

A fair block puzzle. Turn pieces, clear lines, keep the combo. No ads, ever.

Play free in your browser: https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/woven-blocks-site/play/
iPhone & iPad: coming soon to the App Store. Android: later.

- Board: 8 × 8
- Tray: 3 pieces
- Price: Free
- Ads: None
- Account: None
- Network: Plays offline (iPhone, iPad, Android)

## 01 Fair deal: you lose to your moves, never to luck

Every tray of three you are dealt can be placed in full, in some order. If you get stuck, the order was the problem, not luck. The game ends only when no piece fits anywhere, in any turn.

Tap a piece to turn it a quarter turn. Turning is free.

## 02 Score: the score shows its working

Under every clear the game prints the sum that made it, for example `+123 = 3 + 10 × 2² × 3`: three cells placed, two rows cleared, combo ×3.

| Scoring | Points |
|---|---|
| Each cell you place | +1 |
| Each clear | 10 × lines² × combo |
| Empty board, "Perfect" | +300 |

Full rows and columns clear at the same time.

**Keep the combo.** Every placement that clears raises the combo. Clear again within three placements or it resets. The fuse shows how many are left.

## 04 Left alone

Saves after every move. No ads, no account, no tracking. The apps play offline; the web version needs a connection. Light and dark themes, sound and vibration on or off, statistics (best score, games, average, lines, best combo, streaks).

Not in the game: coins, lives, shop, leaderboards, accounts, undo, levels.

| | Many block puzzle apps | Woven Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Ads | Between games, or to continue | None, ever |
| Trays | Random; some can't be placed | Every tray fits, in some order |
| Tracking | Ad and analytics SDKs | None |
| Extras | Coins, lives, boosters | Just the board |
